Warning: Brain wallets created from weak passphrases are extremely insecure. Bots continuously scan these addresses and steal funds within seconds of any deposit. Never use simple passphrases for real wallets!
Bitcoin Brain Wallets

Bitcoin brain wallets convert a passphrase to a private key using SHA-256 hash. The resulting 256-bit hash becomes your secp256k1 private key, from which Legacy (1...), SegWit (bc1q...), or Taproot (bc1p...) addresses are derived.

Due to Bitcoin's popularity, it's the most targeted chain for brain wallet sweepers. Thousands of weak passphrases are monitored 24/7 by automated bots.
